# Dimitri Studio: Vision

**Dimitri Studio is to scientific models what LM Studio is to language models.** It's a simple app that lets researchers run protein structure prediction and other scientific models on their own Mac.

## The problem

Models like AlphaFold, OpenFold and RhoFold are changing biology, but running them still means cloud accounts, GPU clusters, command lines and dependency headaches. Most researchers who would benefit never get past setup.

## What Dimitri Studio is

- **Local-first.** Everything runs on your Mac. No cloud, no uploads, no accounts needed, and your sequences and structures never leave your machine.
- **Model-swappable.** Switch between AlphaFold, OpenFold, RhoFold and other structure-prediction or simulation models in a click, and compare their results side by side on the same input.
- **A model library.** Browse a catalog of scientific models, read what each one is good for, and download new ones with a click, the way LM Studio does for language models.
- **Native to Apple.** A real Mac app built in SwiftUI that feels at home on macOS and makes full use of Apple Silicon.
- **Works natively with LM Studio.** Dimitri Studio runs alongside LM Studio on the same Mac without conflict. It can use LM Studio's local model server, for example so an assistant can explain results or set up runs, and it fits into the same model-library habits, so researchers already using LM Studio feel at home.
- **Made for researchers.** Biologists, chemists and students should be able to go from a sequence to a structure without writing code. Developers are welcome, but they aren't the only audience.

## What success looks like

A researcher downloads Dimitri Studio, picks a model from the library, pastes in a sequence, and sees a 3D structure on their own laptop within minutes. Then they swap in a different model to check the result.

## Principles

1. Private by default: your data stays on your device.
2. Simple first: sensible defaults, with depth available when you want it.
3. Simpler than LM Studio, not a clone of it: fewer screens and fewer settings, with an interface that just works.
4. Open: models and results stay portable and aren't locked into the app.
5. Honest: show what each model can and can't do, and how confident it is.
